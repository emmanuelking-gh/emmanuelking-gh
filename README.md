# Emmanuel King

I build websites, and the small internal systems businesses actually run on.

### spark-prospects-os

- **Spark Prospects OS**: The internal operations console I built for Spark Prospects, replacing their per-event Excel trackers. Website enquiries land in it, deals move through it, and a points board scores whoever is responsible for each delegate. Next.js + Supabase.

### design-lab

My design lab, where client pieces and my own work get designed.

- **[emmanuelking.design](https://emmanuelking.design)**: my own site.
- **[Who Let Da Dogz Out](https://doghousetv.vercel.app)**: an album drop for WLTDO (DOGHOUSE TV). One screen, tap the cover and the record starts.
- **[Tequila & the Bee](https://tequila.tikka.cloud)**: the landing page for a drunk spelling bee in Ibadan and Lagos.

### docrr

- **[docrr](https://docrr.co.uk)**: a portfolio tracker for UK doctors applying to specialty training, plus an eportfolio reflection generator. Next.js + Supabase.

### mafrika-gaming

Games with my brother Xekhai.

- **[tikka-shooter](https://tikka-shooter.vercel.app)**: a hand-drawn 1v1 browser FPS so the two of us can keep a lifetime score. Built on [Doodle District](https://doodleshooter.vercel.app) by Evan Milenko.
- **running-bob**: a third-person action shooter in Unity. You are a stickman and the world is made of paper.

### tools

Desktop apps I built for myself and use every day. Windows, Electron + TypeScript.

- **the-wall**: a bar that hovers on the desktop and opens the others.
- **desk**: my board for everything I'm building, plus to-do, notes, music and life.
- **shotr**: my screenshots folder as a window. Click a shot, click where it goes.
- **yapr**: local transcription for voice notes, system audio and calls.
- **paintr**: paint notes onto a design's filmstrip and get the marks back as text.
