# Emmanuel King

I build websites, and the small internal systems businesses actually run on.

Two halves to the work. One is **operations software** — the console a team opens every morning
instead of a spreadsheet. The other is **one-page pieces**: album drops and event landings where
the whole thing is a single screen and nothing scrolls.

**[emmanuelking.design](https://emmanuelking.design)**

---

### Systems

- **[spark-prospects-os](https://github.com/SparkProspects/spark-prospects-os)** — *Account, Deal,
  Event. One System.* The internal ops console for Spark Prospects, built to retire a stack of
  per-event Excel trackers. Website enquiries land in it, deals move through it, and a points board
  scores whoever is responsible for a delegate. Next.js + Supabase.
- **[docrr](https://github.com/emmanuelking-gh/docrr)** — a portfolio tracker for UK doctors
  applying to specialty training, plus an eportfolio reflection generator, in one app. You pick a
  specialty, enter evidence against the real domains, and it tells you where you are short.
  Next.js + Supabase.

### Pieces

  [source](https://github.com/emmanuelking-gh/who-let-da-dogz-out). An EP release page. One screen,

  drunk spelling bee running two nights, Ibadan and Lagos.
- **[emmanuelking.design](https://emmanuelking.design)** —
  [source](https://github.com/emmanuelking-gh/emmanuelking-design). My own page. Astro, and vanilla

### Games

- **[tikka-shooter](https://github.com/emmanuelking-gh/tikka-shooter)** — a hand-drawn 1v1 browser
  FPS, built so my brother and I could keep a lifetime score. Peer-to-peer over WebRTC with a TURN
  relay for when the routers refuse, two serverless functions and a shared tally.
  Built on [Doodle District](https://github.com/EvanMilenko) by Evan Milenko, with credit on the
  menu. [Play it](https://tikka-shooter.vercel.app).
- **[running-bob](https://github.com/emmanuelking-gh/running-bob)** — a third-person action shooter
  in Unity. You are a stickman and the world is made of paper. Named by me and my brother when we
  were kids, for a game we wanted to make and never made.
